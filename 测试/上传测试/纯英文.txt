The rain fell in sheets across the city. Marcus stood at the window watching the storm roll in from the east. He had been waiting for this night for three years. The code on his screen flickered. Thirty-seven seconds until the transfer would complete. The door burst open. Three men in dark suits stood in the doorway. "Hello Marcus. Long time no see." "Not long enough." The screen flashed green. TRANSFER COMPLETE. Marcus smiled for the first time in three years.
